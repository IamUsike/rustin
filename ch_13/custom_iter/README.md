# Custom iterator

Implement a Fibonacci struct that implements Iterator. It should yield the Fibonacci sequence indefinitely.

Use it with `.take(20)`, `.filter(|n| n % 2 == 0)`, and `.sum::<u64>()`.

No longer human is heavily inspired by Osamu Dazai's own life, and the story follows the life of a main character who cannot understand human interactions, and is completely disconnected from the society. At times he's funny and cheerful, popular with the people around him, but its just a masquerade covering the void that exists within him. He chooses to please people because he scared of them, he's terrified that he cannot understand them, terrified that he'll expose himself, terrified of being shunned.
This pathological people pleasing leads him down a spiral that he can never recover from. This nerve racking story takes us down through the ups and downs of a deeply troubled individual. Knowing that it is based on his own personal life gives the anecdotes much more meaning.
At times, I found myself feeling compassionate at other times I pity the man that osamu is becoming, yelling in my mind "It's Not that Complicated!!!" as he keeps descending, only to realise it's not that simple after all. As the story progresses we see him going down the path of addiction, infidelity, deception etc to a point where we start questioning what does a being a human mean? It gives us a nuanced perspective on life and peeks into the life of a very human person.
